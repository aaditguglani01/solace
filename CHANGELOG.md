# Changelog

## 1.1 (November 2026)
- Renamed to **Solace**
- **Syllabus**: subjects, chapters, revision columns, per-subject and overall progress
- **To-Do**: multiple lists you can rename; completed items move to a Completed section
- **Exams**: countdown in days to your next exam
- **Stats**: study time today, this week, all time, last 7 days, streak, and time per subject
- **Notch timer**: the running timer next to the MacBook notch, with controls on hover
- New timer design, with +5 minutes
- Glow follows Pomodoro (warm for focus, green for breaks) and flashes when a timer ends
- Keyboard shortcuts: Space (start/pause), ⌘N (new to-do), ⌘1–4 (tabs)
- Backup and restore (File › Export Backup / Import Backup)
